Programs Overview

A Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (BSCS) typically spans four years, divided into eight semesters, combining core computer science courses with electives and practical projects.The curriculum covers programming, algorithms, data structures, software engineering, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, and computer systems.Students gain both theoretical foundations and hands-on experience, preparing them for careers in software development, data science, cybersecurity, and research.

Program Objectives
  • Develop a strong foundation in computing fundamentals, mathematics, science, and domain knowledge to effectively solve complex problems.
  • Acquire knowledge and understanding of information security issues in relation to the design, development, and use of information systems.
  • new and emerging technologies such as artificial intelligence, cloud technology, and natural language processing for lifelong learning
  • Work with a range of technologies employed within the game industry, including hand-held devices, desktop devices, and consoles
